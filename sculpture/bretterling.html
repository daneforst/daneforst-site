Sculpture · 07 / Bretterling

Bretterling

Somewhere between an insect and a piece of furniture.

All sculpture

It belongs to nothing in particular, and roughly where it was aiming. The appeal is that you cannot immediately name what you are looking at.

RoleConcept, Sculpt, Art Direction
MediumDigital sculpture
Turns8 clips

The idea

Take two categories that have nothing to do with each other and build the thing that would have to exist between them. The result is uncomfortable in a way that is hard to look away from.

What I would change

The legs, probably. They are the part still arguing with the rest of it.
