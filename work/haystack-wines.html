03 / 08 — Brand Identity + Packaging

Haystack
Wines

coastal character in every detail

RoleBrand Identity / Packaging
ScopeIdentity · Packaging
StudioDEF Studios

Overview

Brand identity and packaging for a wine label with a strong sense of place — character carried from logo to bottle.

Challenge

Stand out on a crowded shelf while feeling timeless rather than trendy — a label you'd want to keep.

Approach

A considered identity system applied to packaging with attention to material, type, and the small details that reward a second look.

Outcome

A label that earns its place on the shelf and the table.
