03 / 08 — Brand Identity + Packaging

Haystack
Wines

coastal character in every detail

RoleBrand Identity / Packaging
ScopeIdentity · Packaging
StudioDEF Studios

Overview

Haystack Wines is a family-owned estate winery in Clements Hills, California — five generations of farming behind every bottle. The work spans a full brand identity and packaging system across three varietal lines: Chardonnay, Syrah, and Rosé, each with its own personality while sharing one coherent mark.

Challenge

Build an identity strong enough to anchor three visually distinct labels — white, dark, and pink — without the system feeling forced. The mark had to work embossed, foil-stamped, and printed, and carry the family's story without becoming a cliché winery brand.

Approach

Developed the interlocking H mark as the cornerstone — geometric, modern, but with enough warmth to carry five generations of family history. Applied across labels with different substrates and finishes: matte white with topographic linework for the whites and rosé, deep black with gold foil for the Syrah. Typography draws from both industrial and classic wine traditions.

Outcome

A cohesive family across three bottles that reads as premium without losing its agricultural roots. The topographic map detail on the back label ties the design to the actual land — Clements Hills AVA — so the story is literally in the label.
