04 / 08 — Exhibition Branding + Experiential

Museum
of Failure

an exhibition built to celebrate the misses

RoleArt Direction / Fabrication
ScopeExhibition · Environmental · Print
StudioDEF Studios

Overview

Museum of Failure is a touring exhibition celebrating 130+ failed products and ideas from around the world — from the ITERA plastic bicycle to New Coke to Elon Musk's greatest hits. The work here covers exhibition branding, environmental graphics, wayfinding, illustrated panel systems, and the physical build-out of the space.

Challenge

Failure is a heavy word. The challenge was building an environment that made people laugh and think simultaneously — where the color system, the typography, and the illustrated characters all pulled toward wit rather than warning. It also needed to tour, so the system had to be modular and adaptable without losing its personality.

Approach

Built a bold three-color system (red, green, yellow) with the X mark as the recurring motif — failure reframed as a symbol you actually want to engage with. Developed the illustrated monkey character as a guide through the exhibition, appearing across panels, signage, and wayfinding. Each panel section had its own visual identity within the system: Digital Disasters, Bad Taste, Failure in Motion, and more. Typography ran large and confident so the show read at exhibition scale.

Outcome

An environment where every surface had a point of view. Visitors moved through a space that felt deliberately designed rather than assembled — the entrance draw, the color blocking, the panel hierarchy, the illustrated moments all working together to make failure genuinely fun to spend time with.
