07 / 09 — Poster Design + Illustration

Tame
Impala

psychedelia, pushed through print

RoleIllustration / Poster Design
ScopePrint · Illustration · Screenprint
StudioDEF Studios

Overview

An official gig poster for Tame Impala's May 16, 2015 show at LC Pavilion in Columbus, OH — designed, illustrated, and printed as a limited-edition piece sold at the venue. The work is entirely hand-conceived: a full illustration built around a surreal central image, typeset with custom letterforms, and printed with the depth and grain of a real physical object.

Challenge

A gig poster has to do two things at once: function as event information and hold up as a standalone piece of art. For a band like Tame Impala — whose visual world is already saturated, psychedelic, and deeply considered — the work had to earn its place in that universe without becoming a reference document of what the band already looks like.

Approach

Started with the central image: an eye emerging from a cracked, levitating pink form — surrealist but grounded, strange but readable. Built the composition around concentric circles and fractured geometry, with the band name set in custom lettering that breaks across the illustration rather than sitting above it. Color palette runs on electric teal, neon pink, and warm orange — screen-print separation baked into the design from the start, so every color layer reads independently before they stack.

Outcome

A limited run printed and sold at the show. The poster moved — and ended up in the hands of people who actually care about the intersection of music and print, which is the only audience worth designing for.
