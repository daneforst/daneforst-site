02 / 08 — Illustration

Club
Tattoo

ink culture, drawn by hand

RoleIllustration
ScopeIllustration · Art Direction
StudioDEF Studios

Overview

Club Tattoo is one of the most recognized tattoo brands in the US, with flagship locations in Las Vegas and Arizona. The illustration work here spans a full merch and apparel system — multiple graphic programs designed to live on bodies, shelves, and storefronts simultaneously.

Challenge

Club Tattoo has a strong visual identity already. The challenge was developing illustration work that felt fresh and distinct across multiple apparel programs — each graphic needing to stand alone while still reading as part of the same world.

Approach

Developed several distinct illustration directions — the diver helmet, the hummingbird, the scorpion, the tiger, the lighter skull — each with its own personality but sharing the same hand-crafted line quality. Every graphic was designed for the specific surface it would live on, from a hat patch to a full chest print to a leather wallet stamp.

Outcome

A merch and apparel system with genuine range — dark and moody, bold and graphic, detailed and collectible. Work that earns a place in the culture rather than just representing it.
