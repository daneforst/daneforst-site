Built from the original Beer Runner files: the hand-drawn sixteen-frame run cycle, the city world strip, the level furniture, and the start and game over screens exactly as they were comped. The rules are the ones printed on that start screen. You need carbonation to run, so grab bubbles, avoid the Hopsters, and hit J to pop a cap in their ass.
