01 · Why this page exists

I already knew how to make things. AI changed what became possible.

I am an Art Director. I have spent eighteen years in branding, advertising, illustration and design, nearly all of it before any of this existed. Generative tools did not teach me composition, casting, color, pacing, or how to tell when an idea is actually finished. They handed me a much larger room to work in.

So this is not a page about AI. It is a page about what happens when somebody who already had a point of view gets a new set of instruments. Almost everything here started as something physical: a costume I built, a puppet I sewed, a 3D print, a photograph, real footage shot on a real day. The machine is the last step, not the first.

Some of it is polished. Some of it is genuinely strange. None of it was made by typing one sentence and accepting the first result.

Tools can automate execution, but they can’t replace perspective and taste.

05 · Three ways to work together
01

Done For You

I handle the creative direction and the production.

You bring the problem or the idea. I work out which tools actually suit it, then direct and produce the thing. You are hiring judgement and a finished result, not a subscription to whichever platform is having a moment.

  • AI campaigns
  • Advertising concepts
  • AI video
  • Social content
  • Brand imagery
  • Concept development
  • Visual experimentation
  • Creative prototyping
Start a project →
02

Done With You

Consulting, working alongside your team.

For companies, creative teams and individuals who want AI inside what they already do but do not know where it fits. Usually the most valuable part of this is deciding where it does not belong, which is a conversation most people are not having.

  • Which tools actually make sense
  • How AI fits an existing workflow
  • Where it saves real time
  • Where it does not make sense
  • Holding visual quality and brand consistency
  • Building repeatable workflows
Talk it through →
03

Teach You

Training, so you stop needing me.

Rather than hiring me to keep making things, learn to make them. You do not need to become an AI expert. You need to know which tools matter for what you are trying to accomplish, and how to tell a good result from a convincing one.

  • One on one sessions
  • Team workshops
  • Custom workflow training
  • Image generation
  • AI video
  • Prompting and creative iteration
  • Using language models well
  • Building an AI assisted process
Ask about training →
